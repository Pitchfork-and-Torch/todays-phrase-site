Today's Phrase support

This app is a daily family code. It does not detect cloned voices.

  • If the Watch is empty, open the iPhone app once after you create or join a house.
  • If two phones show different words, they are not on the same invite, or they do not share the same timezone.
  • If an invite may have leaked, use Settings, rotate secret, and re-invite everyone.
